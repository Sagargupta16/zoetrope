# Kit: S-toy-studio-3d (Pip wants the star)

From the build of 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-11, sixth version: 16.5 s, 1920x1080, 24 fps, a waltz at 120 BPM on recorded instruments. The picture is a 3D plate (`references/plates.md`). Proof plate 153 to 177 s (396 frames at half size); final plate 16.6 to 19.7 min over six renders (2.52 to 2.98 s a frame, Cycles on an RTX 4070 Laptop through OptiX, 64 paths a pixel, 68 MB of frames); `run_all.py full` drew and encoded the 396 frames in 6 to 14 s and made the score in 4 to 22 s (the first run decodes the instruments). 4.8 MB, -14.0 LUFS at -2.0 dBTP, `QA: PASS (50 checks, 0 FIX, 0 NOTE, 0 NOT CHECKED)`, all 13 cues within 5.7 ms on the stem and on their frame in the picture, the catch the loudest moment (momentary -8.9 LUFS, 1.6 LU over the next, the limiter taking 3.0 dB there).

Unlike the older kits, this one carries the registry (`text_items`, `CUES`, `KEY_TIMES`) and passes the gates as it is. Copied onto the engine in a fresh folder it refused to draw before a render (`PLATE: plates/main is not rendered`), passed the proof on the proof plate, and refused `full` on that plate.

| File | What to reuse |
|---|---|
| `beats.py` | The one timeline both worlds read: fps, size, scene lengths, every moment the picture and the sound hit (each a whole frame), how long each jump is in the air, the captions. Start a new film here |
| `stage.py` | `build(st)`: the studio sweep, three out-of-focus hills, the `Buddy`, a prop, and the thing it wants hung where the rig's raised hand will be at the catch (`home = S3.where(hand) + HOLD`), with its own small light and two screen tracks. `pose(t)`: one dict of pose values with defaults, one branch per scene that changes only what that scene needs, then the held prop on the hand and the camera focused on the head. Jumps land on their cue with `S3.jump(t, B.LAND, air)`; a prop that stops on a cue arrives moving, stops dead and rocks; the blink list and the happy eyes; the breath. `contact()` measures the hand-to-star gap frame by frame with no render: `python plate3d.py run stage.py contact` |
| `scenes.py` | `PLATE.frame(t)` as the canvas; `captions(t)` shared by `draw()` and `text_items()`; a caption placed once from a track (`PLATE.at("pip", 8.625)`) so it sits by the character and holds still; sparks that leave from the tracked star every frame, away from the character's face and lamp; the cue list built from `beats.py` |
| `score.py` | `render_audio(scenes, folder, fps)`, which `run_all.py` calls in place of the template score: a waltz bar (`waltz`), tunes and runs on `sampler.py` instruments, one distinct recorded sound per cue label (`cue_sounds`) placed on the SFX bus inside `synth.recording()`, a payoff made loud by held strings under its bells, and the `BED` and `CUE` gains |

To start a film from it: change `beats.py` first (the story's times), restage with stills (`python plate3d.py render stage.py --name look --quality proof --stills T,T`), place anything caught or held from the rig and run the contact measure, step through every jump and catch frame by frame, and keep one sound per cue in `score.py`. A change to `stage.py`, `beats.py` or the engine's `stage3d.py` makes the plate stale, and `run_all.py` says so.

How it got here (every version passed every measured gate):

- **v1** went to a fresh reviewer and came back REVIEW FIX with ten findings. Standing right under the star, Pip's head rose through it at the catch and the star then jumped 177 px to the hand in one frame. The failed jump took the head level with the star, so "Too high." was not true. Two hands raised either side of the star touched nothing. The catch was not the loudest moment. Poses switched across two cuts, and the eyes shut in one frame.
- **v2** fixed all ten in one round. Q902 then noted the crate's picture two frames before its thud: eased gently to rest, a stop has no frame.
- **v3** had the crate arrive still moving, stop dead and rock, and no NOTE was left. A second fresh reviewer found the catch still wrong: moved an arm's reach to the side by worked-out numbers, the hand was inside the star for seven frames before the catch and the star then shifted 46 px onto it. It also found a frown for three frames between the gasp and the smile, takeoffs that snapped, and the crate on screen for only four frames before it stopped.
- **v4** hangs the star where the rig's own hand will be, and measures it: the gap falls from 70.9 cm to 0.3 cm on the frame before the catch, is 0.1 cm from the catch on, is never negative, and the star moves 0.2 cm on the catch frame. The gasp runs into the smile, takeoffs have a push (`stage3d.hop`), and the camera has pulled back before the crate comes in. A third fresh reviewer returned REVIEW PASS on v4 with one P1 and eight P2 notes.
- **v5** fixed all nine in one round. Every landing (2.25, 4.25, 9.875, 11.25, 13.5, 15.0 s) draws its squash on the landing frame: `stage3d.hop` had taken a landing time that fell a float rounding short of the boundary for a frame still in the air. The mouth line hides while the mouth is open (a dot showed on it for one frame at 7.0 and 7.667 s). The sparks start on the catch frame and grow in. The antenna bends in over two frames after each cue (`swing`). The crate is whole on the end card (that camera sits further back and lower). The hop's landing sound is at gain 0.55, and the held strings under the catch at 0.34 with the last harp run at 0.16, which makes the catch the loudest moment by 1.7 LU (0.6 before). The star's lamp is back under the star and skips the holding hand and arm (`st.unlit`): moved to the side it still turned the hand bronze, and moved above and in front it washed the star pale. A fourth fresh reviewer stepped through every jump, the catch, the crate and the face frame by frame and returned REVIEW PASS with no P0 or P1 and three P2 notes.
- **v6** fixed those three. The three short hops (the hop and both bounces) crouch over three frames and push over three, where they dipped in one (`S3.jump` gives a hop under half a second in the air a crouch of `wind` seconds; the flinch at the crate keeps `wind=0`). A soft rush of air starts at 6.71 s, two frames before Pip turns to look, where nothing sounded until 7.0 s. In the reach scene the camera pulls back 0.4 m as it tilts up and comes down behind Pip after the catch, so the crate is whole from the landing to the cut. A fifth fresh reviewer compared it with v5 frame by frame (they differ only in those windows), returned REVIEW PASS with no P0 or P1, and `qa.py approve` bound it to the file (`VERIFY: SAME`). These files are that build.

What that review still noted on v6 (five P2, two of them marked accept), for whoever builds from this kit next:

- The rush before the crate is one sound in two stages: it rises to 6.96 s, sags 3.6 dB by 7.12 s and swells again to the stop. Make the first breath `S.whoosh(0.6, seed=8)` with its peak at `B.CRATE_IN + 0.16`, and check that no 40 ms block between 6.96 and 7.40 s is more than 2 dB under the one before. Audio only.
- The push of the three short hops puts most of its travel on one frame (the head rises 4 px, then 47): `stage3d.hop` eases the push in and out. For a softer push, use the plain share when the push is half the crouch. It changes the plate.
- On the landing frame at 11.25 s the crate is whole but its base is 5 px from the bottom edge (27 px a frame later). Accept, or start `fall` one frame earlier (`B.TOUCH + 0.043`) and check the sparks at the top edge again.
- One spark crosses the holding arm for three frames after the catch (10.917 to 11.0 s): in `sparkles()`, `260` to `225` ends the arc above the hand.
- The holding hand's top third is amber while the star flashes and its lower half stays indigo: it reads as the star's light on a glossy hand. Accept, or lower the flash term `2.0` in the stage's `glow` to about 1.2.
- Not judged: the jumps at speed (they were stepped, not played), and the sound by ear.

Other lessons:

- A camera that comes down ahead of the character lifts what it holds in the frame: with the fall eased from the catch, the star rose 40 px and three sparks left by the top edge. Started 0.085 s after the catch and run over 0.5 s, it stays behind Pip.
- The master's limiter may take 0.5 LU (Q805). This score sits at 0.46 to 0.49: adding the quiet rush moved it from 0.48 to 0.50 and a FIX, and the held strings went from 0.34 to 0.32 to give it room (0.47).
- The first staging had the character at a fifth of the frame height in three scenes; the camera came in and the star came down.
- A glow of 1.2 on the star and a light in front of it washed the gold to white: the glow is 0.15 at rest, 2.0 for the flash, and the light sits under the star. From there it also lit the hand 12 cm away gold, so it skips the hand and arm (`st.unlit`); what warmth is left on the hand is the star's reflection.
- Bells alone are all peak: at the gain that made the catch loudest, the limiter took 6.5 dB (Q805 FIX), and more plucked notes on top raised the peak again. Held violins and cellos under the bells made it the loudest moment with the limiter at 2.6 dB.
- A stretch of 0.22 on the reach jump lifted the hand to the star's height three frames early: that jump uses 0.1.
- Sustained strings, flute and horn swell in: they are placed by their first sample here as a bed, which is fine under a tune, but a string note that must hit a cue needs `align="pad"` (`references/instruments.md`).
- Nobody has listened to this score. Its balance was set by measurement.
- Not built: motion blur, a 9:16 staging (the same stage renders at 1080x1920 but crops the character in two scenes), a second character on screen.
