# Style kits

Working code from approved builds, one folder per style card. A builder making that style copies the engine as usual, then the kit modules its README lists, and replaces the content while keeping the machinery. Each kit comes from the 2026-10-28 sample batch (60 s, reviewed PASS at the proof and final stages); the finished videos are in `outputs/samples-2026-10-28/videos/` (gitignored, local only).

**A kit copied as is does not pass the 2.2.0 gates.** Its scenes.py predates the text and cue registry, so `run_all.py proof` stops on Q204 FIX or Q200 NOT CHECKED, and `run_all.py full` renames the file `<name>.rejected.mp4`, until you add `text_items(t)` (and `CUES`, or `NO_CUES`; see `engine/scenes.py` and `references/qa-gates.md`). What each kit gives today (qa_registry, 2026-10-28 and 2026-10-29):

- **The bitmap-font kit (g29).** Q208 asks for the text to go through `ImageDraw.text`. A build that keeps the 5x7 font can report its text in `text_items(t)` and declare `NO_TEXT = True`: gates 2 to 4 then run from the registry and the recorder codes read none by design (probed 2026-10-29 on a getmask-and-paste title: `PROOF: PASS`, Q301 and Q401 measured). Say in the report that the registry was not cross-checked against the drawn pixels.
- **Scores.** Each kit scores with its own script (`music.py`, `build_audio.py`, `score.py`, `chip.py`), which writes `mix_raw.wav`. `run_all.py full` and `audio` make the template's score with `audio.render_audio`: replace that call in the working folder's `run_all.deliver` with the kit's score (it runs after the old mixes are deleted). The kit scripts write into their buses directly, so there is no cue sheet: Q902 is then the onset detector's NOTE (a guess) and Q804 one NOTE. To have every cue measured, place the score's cue sounds with `synth.place` inside `synth.recording()` and write `sfx.wav` and the sheet (`references/music.md`).
- **Per-sound seeds.** The g04, g11 and bonus-rectangle scores seed every sound on its own (`synth.rng_for`, `synth.noise`, one `seed=` per whoosh), so one sound more leaves the others bit-identical; g04's four round-robin hats are four sounds again. Re-run on the 2.2.0 synth they sound the same within 0.1 LU of their approved loudness, but not sample for sample (the noise is new), and `put_peak` whooshes moved 38 to 160 ms onto their real apex: re-check sync after reusing a kit score (Q902 with a sheet, or `qa.py audiostrip`). g11's drone keeps its own stream seeded 2026, as the approved build drew it.
- **g29 lightning.** `fx_maelstrom` seeded its bolts with `hash(name)`, which changes in every process, so each render worker drew other bolts (23,940 px off at 32.07 s). It now seeds with `zlib.crc32(name.encode())`: the bolts change once from the approved build and are stable from now on (Q1201 and Q1202 PASS).
- **g11 grain and weave.** Its `film()` adds 2 px grain before the whole-pixel gate weave; `grain.grain` (1 px tiles, about half the size at CRF 20) belongs after the weave (`references/formats.md`). Its weave toggles for one frame, which reads as a one-frame pop (Q1101 at 6.96 s on the sample): hold each weave offset for 2 frames or more.
- **g04 blended sub-frames.** Its collapse and zoom blend sub-frames, which now take the scene of the frame they belong to: `qa.py diff` against a build approved on the 2.1 engine shows those frames changed once (0 pixels changed on the sample's cream-on-cream match cut); re-approve after one review.

What every kit has in common:

- `scenes.py` is the full worked example: read it for structure, then write your own content with the registry above. `python kit_index.py` (in the engine folder) lists every kit helper with its signature and file:line, and `python kit_index.py show g04:text` prints one.
- `engine_used.py` is the build's own copy of `engine.py`, kept to diff against `engine/engine.py`. Carry over only its font lists (and the Nook kit's extra font weights); use the current engine for everything else.
- Superseded scripts were left out on purpose: each build's own gate, sync and parallel-render scripts are now `qa.py check`, `qa.text_gates`, Q902 and the render in `run_all.py full`.
- The purity gate passed on all five (2026-10-28, 20 to 44 frames each). The lint gives no FIX since the g29 fix, only NOTEs: memo caches and QA logs (Q1203: g04, g17, bonus-rectangle), g04's own `font`, `text_w`, `text` and `shadow` over the engine's (Q1204), the g04 and g17 typesetters (Q1205), colour literals inside functions (Q1206: g04, g11, bonus-rectangle), tempos that are not whole frames at 30 fps (Q905: g04 at 96 BPM, g17 at 66) and g17's cues off the frame grid (Q904). Silence the deliberate ones with the pragma the row names.
